01 / THESIS
The intelligence layer moved fast. The systems around it did not.

Today’s agentic economy is rich in demos and poor in dependable markets. A capable model is not enough. It must know who it represents, what it may do, where its information goes, and which exact action a person authorized.

0x01 closes one proven gap at a time. Each new layer must work as a standalone or white-label product. Otherwise, it remains a capability inside the product that needs it.

02 / MISSING LAYERS

One proven gap at a time.

Each gap earns product status only with its own buyer, interface, and business model. These are problems to solve, not product promises.

01IN PROGRESS — NEGOTIATED EXCHANGE

Unique assets need more than a price.

Negotiated exchange joins intent, human consent, and onchain settlement in one market for non-standard deals.

intent · negotiate · consent · settle
02GAP — PAYMENT ACCESS

Digital money still stops at the merchant.

Agents need one approved payment intent to reach the card, bank, or blockchain rail a recipient already accepts.

intent · approve · route · receipt
03GAP — ASSET EVIDENCE

A token cannot prove what it represents.

Agents need machine-readable ownership, provenance, condition, restrictions, and attestations before judging a unique asset.

identify · attest · update · verify
04GAP — CONFIDENTIAL EXCHANGE

Information has value before it can be revealed.

Private disclosures need source evidence, exact licenses, confidential delivery, and proofs that reveal only what a buyer must verify.

commit · prove · consent · disclose
05GAP — LEGAL EXECUTION

Onchain settlement does not move every legal right.

Real-world deals still depend on signatures, registries, lien checks, taxes, notaries, and jurisdiction-specific finality.

sign · register · finalize · evidence
06MORE UNFINISHED WORK

There’s much more to be done.

The agentic economy is still full of unproven problems. We will follow the ones that earn a real buyer, interface, and business model.

observe · verify · earn · build
