A new thesis from 0x01

We were early.
Now we’re building for people.

The technology moved fast. Human reasons to trust it did not. We are rebuilding around the things people can actually use, own, understand, and control.

Why we changed Follow the work
Human agency Legible ownership Better coordination
01 / THE RESET
A capable system is not the same thing as a useful one.

We began with a belief that autonomous agents were ready to become economic actors. The capability is real; the broad human demand is not there yet.

The better question is not how quickly a system can act without us. It is how clearly it can extend our intent without taking away our agency.

02 / PRINCIPLES

The person remains the principal.

We are building from practical human needs outward—not from a capability looking for a market.

01CONTROL

Authority must stay visible.

People need to know who can act, what they can do, and how that permission can end.

02OWNERSHIP

What you hold should be legible.

Rights, obligations, and provenance should be understandable before they are programmable.

03COORDINATION

Better agreements beat louder promises.

Useful systems make it easier to propose, negotiate, verify, and carry out a shared decision.

Manifesto / in progress

We are putting the original idea in its right place.

Software can prepare, inform, and operate within clear limits. But people should keep the authority to decide, to own, and to change their mind.

That is the new 0x01: systems for human-controlled coordination, ownership, and exchange. We will publish the full manifesto when the work is ready to be held to it.
