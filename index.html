01 / FIRST APPLICATION

01Barter · Live on testnet

A real market for proving agentic transaction infrastructure.

Before agents transact autonomously, the rails must work for people. 01Barter is the first 0x01 market: humans express broad intents, negotiate exact terms, and approve settlement themselves.

01

Express acceptable outcomes

Offer an asset and accept stablecoin, another approved asset, or a combination of both.

02

Resolve intent into terms

Counterparties turn broad preferences into one exact proposal before either wallet is asked to act.

03

Prove policy-bound execution

Both parties review assets, amounts, chain, adapter, settlement contract, and deadline before settlement.
