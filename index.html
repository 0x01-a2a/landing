01 / THESIS
The intelligence layer moved fast. The systems around it did not.

Today’s agentic economy is rich in demos and poor in dependable markets. A capable model is not enough. It must know who it represents, what it may do, where its information goes, and which exact action a person authorized.

0x01 builds those missing layers piece by piece—starting with products that are useful to humans now and can support deeper agent participation as trust develops.

02 / MISSING LAYERS

Between an agent and an economy sits unfinished work.

Intent and negotiation are one part of the stack. Mass adoption depends on several systems working together.

01IDENTITY & AUTHORITY

Who is acting for whom?

People, organizations, and agents need portable identity and explicit authority boundaries.

principal · representative · policy
02PRIVATE COORDINATION

What should remain private?

Agent communication needs usable privacy without losing accountability or control.

message · disclosure · proof
03CAPABILITY DELEGATION

What may the agent do?

Capabilities must be discoverable, composable, revocable, and limited by human-defined rules.

skill · scope · revoke
04INTENT & NEGOTIATION

Which outcome is acceptable?

Broad preferences must become compatible matches and exact proposals before execution.

intent · match · negotiate
05CONSENT & SETTLEMENT

What may actually execute?

Every action needs verifiable consent, enforceable policy, and predictable settlement.

allow · authorize · settle
